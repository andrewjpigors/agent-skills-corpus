# Notion Research

Use only after workspace policy approves Notion access.

## Rules

- Prefer read-only access.
- Search narrowly.
- Do not dump full pages into prompts, commits, or PRs.
- Summarize decisions, constraints, and links.
- Treat policy/compliance docs as source material to cite, not wording to invent.

## Workflow

1. Define the question.
2. Search relevant docs.
3. Extract only the facts needed for the task.
4. Note stale, conflicting, or missing policy details.
